Rin - VN Sprite Sampler
=======================

10 transparent-PNG character sprites of Rin, 832x1248.

Usage
  Drop the PNGs into your VN project (Ren'Py: game/images/). They are straight
  alpha, no premultiplication, no matte colour to key out.

License
  Royalty-free for commercial and non-commercial visual novels and games.

  or redistribute the sprites as an asset pack.

AI disclosure
  These sprites were generated with AI image models (Qwen-Image) and reviewed
  by hand. Declared as AI-generated on the store listing per itch.io policy.

Getting started
  renpy_example.rpy in this folder declares every sprite and shows how to swap
  expressions and outfits, including a scaling transform that fits them to a
  720p screen without resizing the files.

What this sampler leaves out
  These 10 sprites are a taste, not a matrix: 7 expressions in the base
  outfit, plus 3 alternate outfit looks. The full pack is the complete
  outfit x expression grid -- every outfit combined with every expression,
  28 sprites per character -- and it covers BOTH characters, Rin and Mika,
  for 56 in total. It also includes each character's signature outfit, which
  is not in this sampler at all.

  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/rin-vn-sprite-full-pack

  Same style, same naming scheme, same straight-alpha cutouts as the files you
  just downloaded, so nothing about your setup has to change.

Aseprite tools from the same workshop
  If you also do pixel art, these are the extensions built alongside these
  sprites:

    AutoBlob      47-tile blob tilesets from a handful of drawn tiles
                  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/autoblob
    EdgeForge     outlines and drop shadows that respect transparency
                  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/edgeforge
    PaletteGuard  audits a sprite against a locked palette
                  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/paletteguard
    CelTween      motion tweening between cels
                  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/celtween
    DitherForge   dithered gradients from real Bayer/noise matrices
                  https://violetpixel13.itch.io/ditherforge

  Two are free and need no account at all:

    Palette Extractor -- exact palettes from any sprite, in your browser
                  https://pixelpixi.github.io/spritewright/palette-extractor/
    PaletteOut -- Aseprite extension, MIT licensed
                  https://github.com/pixelpixi/aseprite-extension-starter
